Executive Summary & Assessment Architecture

This official curriculum assessment repository establishes the pedagogical alignment between instructional competencies, cognitive skill domains, and evaluation instruments for Information Technology 10 at the MSU-IIT Integrated Developmental School. It encompasses both formative classroom worksheets (quizzes) and the summative 1st Quarter Examination.

  • Formative Worksheets (Quizzes 1–5): 10-minute timed assessments featuring randomized draws, SVG resistor diagrams, and rubric-scored Virtual Arduino Lab simulations.
  • Summative 1st Quarter Examination: 45-minute comprehensive exam utilizing a 77-item bank. Each student receives 50 items (12 mandatory items: 10 visual component identifications + 2 laboratory problem-solving tasks, plus 38 unseen-first items).
  • DepEd Alignment: Structured across Remembering (Recall), Understanding (Comprehension), Applying (Computation/Decoding), and Analyzing/Creating (Circuit Design & Code Synthesis).

Assessment Administration & Test Security Policy

Under the approved IT 10 assessment guidelines (SY 2026–2027), all examinations and quizzes adhere to the following execution protocols:

  • Fixed Window Gating: Assessments open strictly within scheduled timeframes (e.g., Friday 08:00–10:00 Asia/Manila). Submissions outside the window are server-blocked.
  • Randomized Unseen-First Draw: Each quiz draws a fresh 20-item subset from its authored bank, while the exam draws 50 items (12 mandatory + 38 randomized), guaranteeing integrity.
  • Grading & Review Policy: Immediate score visibility upon submission; answer keys and detailed explanations are withheld until official teacher release.
