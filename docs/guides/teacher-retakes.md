# Teacher worksheet retakes

Students receive one attempt on each quiz or exam by default. After submitting their last available attempt, a student can request another attempt from the worksheet list. The request appears in that worksheet's teacher attempt roster for approval or denial.

## Student steps

After a worksheet is submitted and no attempts remain, the student selects **Request retake** beside it. The portal shows **Request pending** while the teacher reviews it. If approved, the normal start action becomes available again; if denied, the student may submit a new request later.

1. Open the course worksheet and its attempt roster.
2. Review pending retake requests at the top of the roster.
3. Select **Approve retake** to grant one attempt, or **Deny** to decline. Teachers can also select **Grant next attempt** directly from the student's row.
4. After approval, the student can reopen the worksheet from the student portal. Their earlier submission remains in the attempt history.

Requests and decisions are retained with their status. Each approval, denial, manual grant, or reset is recorded in the staff audit log with the teacher, worksheet, and student. **Reset attempts** deletes the student's attempt history and removes their grants; use it only when the history should be cleared.
